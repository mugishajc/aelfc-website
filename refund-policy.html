Refund Policy

This policy covers paid coaching and advisory sessions booked through this website.

Booking & Payment

Sessions are confirmed once payment has been made and a slot has been agreed with our team. Payment secures your booking time.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

If you need to reschedule, contact us at least 24 hours before your scheduled session and we will work with you to find a new time at no extra cost.

Refunds

Because sessions involve dedicated preparation and reserved time, fees are generally non-refundable once a session has taken place. If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice or do not attend, the session fee is forfeited. If AELFC Ltd is unable to deliver a session you have paid for, you will receive a full refund or a rescheduled session, at your choice.
