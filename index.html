About Us

Anaclet's Experts Legal & Financial Consultancy Ltd

AELFC Ltd is a professional consulting firm based in Kigali, Rwanda, delivering comprehensive legal, financial and business solutions to individuals, businesses, NGOs and public institutions.

We combine legal expertise, financial excellence and strategic advisory services to help our clients achieve compliance, improve performance, manage risks and attain sustainable growth. Every client deserves reliable, practical and innovative solutions tailored to their unique needs, delivered by a multidisciplinary team committed to professionalism, integrity and confidentiality.

Our core areas of expertise include Legal Advisory, Accounting & Bookkeeping, Tax Consultancy, Internal & External Audit, Financial Advisory, Business & Management Consultancy, Mediation & Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR), Research & Training, and Capacity Building. We work closely with our clients to strengthen governance, improve financial management, resolve disputes amicably and support informed decision-making.

Meet the Founder

Anaclet Mugisha

Founder & Managing Director | Legal, Financial & Business Consultant

Anaclet Mugisha is the Founder and Managing Director of AELFC Ltd, a multidisciplinary consultancy firm providing professional legal, financial, business and mediation services in Rwanda. He is a dedicated legal and financial professional with expertise in corporate governance, accounting, auditing, taxation, financial management, compliance and alternative dispute resolution.

He holds a Bachelor of Science in Accounting from the University of Rwanda and is a Certified Public Finance Manager (CPFM) and a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.). He is also pursuing a Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Finance at the University of Lay Adventists of Kigali (UNILAK), while advancing toward the Certified Public Accountant (CPA) qualification at the Intermediate Level.

Throughout his career, Anaclet has served in key positions including Chief Accountant, Internal Auditor, Financial Consultant and Accounting Professional, supporting government institutions, private companies and non-governmental organizations. He also leads professional training and capacity-building initiatives in accounting, finance, taxation, auditing, legal practice, entrepreneurship and business management.
